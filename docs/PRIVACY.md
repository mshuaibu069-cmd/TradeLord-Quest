# TradeLord Quest Privacy Policy — Draft

**Version:** 2026-10-02

This is an implementation draft, not legal advice or the final public policy. It must be reviewed against the laws of every market where the app is offered.

## What TradeLord Quest is
TradeLord Quest is an educational trading simulator. It uses virtual money and is not a real-money trading service.

## Data we may process
Depending on features used, we may process:
- account email and authentication identifiers;
- display name and profile information;
- virtual balance, points, streaks, lessons, challenges, and simulator activity;
- support complaints and messages;
- security events such as failed sign-ins, abuse signals, and system errors;
- subscription and purchase information when Premium is introduced;
- optional product analytics only when enabled.

We should not collect location, contacts, microphone, camera, or other sensitive device data unless a future feature genuinely requires it and the privacy notice is updated first.

## Why we process data
We use data to:
- provide accounts and the simulator;
- save learning progress;
- provide support;
- protect accounts and the service from abuse and fraud;
- operate Premium features;
- improve the product when optional analytics are enabled.

## Automated support
TradeLord Quest may use an automated support system to classify complaints, summarize routine issues, suggest responses, and identify cases that need human review.

Automated support must not secretly monitor users. Security monitoring should be limited to information necessary to protect the service and disclosed to users.

High-impact actions such as permanent account deletion, financial changes, or serious enforcement should use controlled server-side rules and, where appropriate, human review.

## Sharing
We should not sell personal data. Data may be processed by service providers needed to operate the app, such as authentication, database, hosting, analytics (if enabled), payments, and AI support providers. Third-party processing must be covered by appropriate contracts and disclosures.

## Security
We use server-side authorization, row-level security, encrypted connections, secret management, rate limits, audit logs, and restricted administrative access. No security design can guarantee that hacking is impossible.

AI API keys and other private credentials must never be placed in the mobile application.

## Retention and deletion
Users can request account and associated data deletion. Certain limited records may need to be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, dispute handling, or legal obligations.

## User rights
Subject to applicable law, users may have rights to be informed, access their data, correct it, object or restrict processing, request portability, and request erasure.

## Complaints
Users can contact TradeLord Quest through the in-app Help & Complaints system. Privacy complaints can be escalated for human review.

## Compliance and risk
The service should comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other privacy/data-protection laws that apply in each market. Privacy notices, consent, processor agreements, retention rules, security controls, breach procedures, and Google Play disclosures must stay synchronized with the actual implementation. A privacy policy does not prevent lawsuits or regulatory action by itself; compliance and evidence of responsible data handling are the protection.

## Changes
We will update this policy when the app's data practices change.

**Before Google Play release:** replace this draft with a legally reviewed public privacy policy at a permanent HTTPS URL and keep it synchronized with Google Play Data Safety declarations.
